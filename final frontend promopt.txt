DISISTA CONTROL — Role-Based Access
1. Control Room

Purpose: Central command and decision-making.

Access:

Dashboard — Full access
Live Map — Full access
Convoy Dispatch — Create, monitor, reroute, and manage convoys
Shelter Board — Full network visibility
Hazard Log — View, verify, and manage hazards
Supply Swap — Manage and coordinate transfers
Alerts — View, acknowledge, and escalate critical alerts
Warehouse Inventory — Network-wide visibility
Road Blocking — Block road segments and broadcast rerouting
System Settings — Full operational settings
Reports and network analytics — Full access

Main responsibility:
Monitor the complete disaster network, make operational decisions, coordinate resources, and escalate critical situations.

2. District Admin

Purpose: Manage and coordinate relief operations within a specific district.

Access:

Dashboard — District-level overview
Live Map — View district operations
Convoy Dispatch — Monitor and coordinate district convoys
Shelter Board — View district shelters and shortages
Hazard Log — View and verify district hazards
Supply Swap — Coordinate and approve district-level transfers
Alerts — District alerts and escalation
Warehouse Inventory — View district warehouses
Reports — District-level reports
Settings — Role-specific settings

Main responsibility:
Coordinate warehouses, shelters, hazards, and convoys within the assigned district and escalate major problems to the Control Room.

3. Warehouse Manager

Purpose: Manage warehouse inventory and ensure critical supplies reach locations where they are needed.

Access:

Dashboard — Warehouse-focused overview
Live Map — View relevant warehouses, shelters, hazards, and supply routes
Convoy Dispatch — View supply-related convoys and delivery status
Shelter Board — View shelter demand and shortages
Hazard Log — View relevant hazards
Supply Swap — Full access
Alerts — Warehouse and supply-related alerts
Inventory — Full access to own warehouse
Settings — Personal/warehouse settings

Can perform:

Check available inventory
Identify surplus supplies
Identify critical shortages
Offer surplus through Supply Swap
Request supplies from another warehouse
Monitor supply transfers
Track incoming/outgoing convoys
Check shelter demand
Respond to critical inventory alerts

Cannot perform:

Block roads
Manage the entire convoy network
Modify verified field hazard reports
Manage other warehouses without authorization
Perform system-wide administration

Main responsibility:
Monitor inventory → Identify shortage/surplus → Supply Swap → Coordinate transfer → Track delivery → Confirm availability

Access:

Acknowledge instructions

Confirm delivery

Modify warehouse inventory

District Admin — Features in Simple Language
1. 📊 Dashboard

See the overall situation of the district in one place.

How many shelters need help
How many convoys are active
Current hazards
Available supplies
Critical alerts
District risk status
2. 🗺️ Live Map

See everything happening inside the assigned district on a map.

Locations of shelters
Warehouses
Active convoys
Floods and landslides
Blocked or unsafe roads
Areas requiring urgent help
3. 🚛 Convoy Dispatch

Monitor and coordinate relief vehicles operating in the district.

See which convoys are moving
Check their destination
Check their ETA
See if a convoy is delayed or at risk
Coordinate rerouting when necessary

Example:
A convoy going to Shelter A is delayed because of flooding → District Admin checks the situation and coordinates an alternative route with the Control Room.

4. 🏠 Shelter Board

Check the condition and supply needs of shelters in the district.

Which shelters are safe
Which shelters have low supplies
Which shelters are critical
What supplies they need
How urgently they need them
Whether a convoy is coming

Example:
Shelter B has only 1 day of water remaining → District Admin can identify it as a priority.

5. ⚠️ Hazard Log

View and verify hazards reported in the district.

Examples:

Flood
Landslide
Damaged bridge
Blocked road
Debris

The Admin can check whether a reported hazard is genuine and important enough to affect operations.

6. 🔄 Supply Swap

Help move supplies from a location with extra stock to a location with a shortage.

Example:

Warehouse A has extra water
↓
Warehouse B has low water
↓
District Admin coordinates the Supply Swap

This helps prevent one warehouse from having unused surplus while another location faces a shortage.

7. 🚨 Alerts

See important problems happening in the district.

Examples:

Critical shelter shortage
Convoy delayed
Road blocked
New major hazard
Critical medical supply shortage

The District Admin can acknowledge important alerts and escalate serious problems to the Control Room.

8. 📦 Warehouse Inventory

View the warehouses operating inside the district.

The Admin can see:

Available supplies
Low-stock items
Critical items
Incoming supplies
Outgoing supplies
Supply transfers

Important: The District Admin mainly monitors warehouse information rather than directly managing every warehouse's inventory.

9. 📈 Reports

Get a summary of what happened in the district.

Reports can show:

Number of deliveries
Delayed convoys
Critical shelters
Reported hazards
Supply movements
Resolved alerts
Overall district performance

This helps the Admin understand what is improving and what still needs attention.

10. ⚙️ Settings

Manage settings related to the Admin's role.

For example:

Account information
Language
Notification preferences
Display preferences
Operational preferences
🎯 In Simple Words

The District Admin acts as the middle-level coordinator.

Control Room = Controls the overall network
District Admin = Manages one district
Warehouse Manager = Manages supplies

District Admin's Main Workflow

Monitor District → Find Problem → Coordinate Resources → Handle the Situation → Escalate Serious Problems → Confirm Resolution



3Warehouse Manager — Simple Feature Description
Purpose

The Warehouse Manager manages the supplies in their warehouse and makes sure the right supplies are sent where they are needed.

1. 📊 Dashboard

Shows the current status of their warehouse.

Total supplies
Low-stock supplies
Critical supplies
Extra/available supplies
Incoming deliveries
Outgoing deliveries
Important alerts
2. 🗺️ Live Map

Shows locations related to their warehouse.

Their warehouse
Other relevant warehouses
Shelters
Convoys
Hazards
Supply routes

The manager can understand where supplies are going and whether there are problems on the route.

3. 🚛 Convoy Dispatch

Shows convoys carrying supplies from or to their warehouse.

The manager can:

See convoy status
See destination
Check ETA
See delayed deliveries
Track incoming supplies
Track outgoing supplies

They cannot control all convoys.

4. 🏠 Shelter Board

Shows what shelters need.

For each shelter, the manager can see:

Required supply
Required quantity
Current supply level
Urgency
Incoming supply

Example:
Shelter A needs 500 units of water → Manager can check whether their warehouse can provide it.

5. ⚠️ Hazard Log

Shows hazards that may affect their warehouse or supply routes.

Examples:

Flood
Landslide
Damaged bridge
Blocked road

The manager can view relevant hazards, but cannot modify verified hazard information.

6. 🔄 Supply Swap

This is one of the main features for the Warehouse Manager.

The manager can:

See extra supplies
Offer extra supplies
See requests from other warehouses
Request supplies they don't have
Accept/coordinate a supply transfer
Track the transfer

Simple example:

Warehouse A has 1,000 water bottles
Warehouse B needs 500
↓
Warehouse A offers 500
↓
Supply Swap created
↓
Convoy transports the supplies
↓
Warehouse B receives them

7. 🚨 Alerts

Shows alerts related to the warehouse.

Examples:

Supply running low
Critical item shortage
Incoming delivery delayed
Outgoing delivery delayed
Supply Swap request
Important route problem

The manager can read and respond to relevant alerts.

8. 📦 Inventory

This is the main working area for the Warehouse Manager.

The manager can:

View all stock
Check available quantity
Check reserved quantity
Identify low stock
Identify surplus
Track incoming stock
Track outgoing stock
Update warehouse inventory when authorized
Important:

Available supply = Total stock − Reserved supply

This prevents the manager from offering supplies that are already reserved for another operation.

9. ⚙️ Settings

Basic settings for the manager.

Account details
Warehouse information
Notification preferences
Language
Display preferences
❌ What the Warehouse Manager Cannot Do

To keep roles clear:

❌ Cannot block roads
❌ Cannot control all convoys
❌ Cannot change verified hazards
❌ Cannot manage other warehouses directly
❌ Cannot change system-wide settings
❌ Cannot manage users or roles
🔄 Simple Warehouse Manager Workflow

        ↓

        ↓
